CDK Terrain v0.23.0 brought [mixins](/concepts/mixins) to CDKTN projects. A
mixin applies a reusable piece of behavior to one specific construct, without
subclassing it.

## What changed

### `constructs` peer dependency raised to `^10.6.0`

v0.23.0 raised the [`constructs`](https://github.com/aws/constructs) peer
dependency from `^10.4.2` to `^10.6.0`
([cdk-terrain#20](https://github.com/open-constructs/cdk-terrain/pull/20)).
`constructs` 10.6.0 adds the mixin primitive, and CDKTN inherits it from that
library:

- The `IMixin` interface, with a `supports(construct)` method that decides
  whether the mixin applies to a construct and an `applyTo(construct)` method
  that applies the change.
- The `construct.with(...mixins)` method, which applies mixins to a construct
  immediately and returns the same construct.

If your project pins `constructs` below 10.6.0, raise it when you upgrade to
v0.23.

### No built-in mixins yet

CDKTN core does not ship ready-made mixins. You write your own, using building
blocks that work with any provider, such as `TerraformResource.isTerraformResource`
and `addOverride`.

## Learn more

The [Mixins concept page](/concepts/mixins) shows how to define and apply
mixins, with examples. It also explains when to use a mixin instead of an
[Aspect](/concepts/aspects).
